SpeakFree Keyboard Privacy Policy

Effective August 16, 2026

SpeakFree Keyboard is designed to process typing and swipe gestures on your device.

Data collection

The SpeakFree Keyboard iOS app and keyboard extension do not collect, store, sell, or share personal data. They do not include advertising, analytics, tracking, accounts, or telemetry.

Keyboard and swipe data

Tap and swipe input is processed locally on your device. The keyboard does not transmit keystrokes, swipe paths, typed text, document context, or model results to SpeakFree or any third party.

Full Access and networking

The keyboard works without “Allow Full Access.” Its iOS extension configuration does not request open network access.

Local resources

The keyboard uses a model and word-frequency vocabulary bundled with the app. Current iOS V1 behavior does not create a personal dictionary or retain typing history.

Apple services

Apple may process App Store downloads, TestFlight diagnostics, or crash reports under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy. SpeakFree does not receive data from those services unless a user separately chooses to share it through Apple.

Changes

If SpeakFree Keyboard later adds an optional feature that collects or transmits data, this policy and the app’s disclosures will be updated before that feature is released.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests may be submitted through the SpeakFree issue tracker.
