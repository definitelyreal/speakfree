## speakfree v1.7.2

- **Clearer recordings choices.** Keep Recordings & Transcripts offers Keep All, Keep None, Last 1,000, and Last 10,000. First-time setup asks before saving; capped retention explains its permanent cleanup behavior.
- **Trash and Restore.** Moving existing recordings to Trash happens in the background, with progress and a Restore action. Recordings in use stay put. Restore preserves newer files, reports recordings separately from the number of files, and recognizes batches returned with Finder's Put Back.
- **More consistent Settings.** Aligned controls, clearer usage totals, vocabulary editing in Transcription, compact model-readiness status, and a full-size rounded folder button with a yellow icon. Parakeet no longer exposes unsupported live-preview or Whisper-only model-loading controls.
- **Mac Screen Sharing insertion.** Remote text delivery now uses the remote-app insertion path instead of the event path that could produce repeated “a” characters. Failed delivery offers recoverable text without an automatic retry into another app.
- **Microphone and transcription reliability.** Recovery improvements for stopped or silent capture engines, better preservation of recording tails and quick follow-on takes, and safer background model cleanup.

Apple Silicon, macOS 14 or later. Speech recognition remains on-device. AirPods microphone use still switches Bluetooth into its lower-quality call-audio mode.

AI-assistant skill integration is planned for a separate update and is not included here.
