## speakfree v1.7.2

- **Your recordings are a corpus, not a confession.** The notice about locally saved dictations now explains what an archive of your own speech can do for you (replaying accuracy changes, finding the words that keep going wrong, teaching your vocabulary). Saving stays off unless you turn it on, everything stays on your Mac, and deleting is still one click.
- **Fix:** switching the engine to Whisper no longer crashes the app in a loop.
- **Fix:** AirPods and other Bluetooth mics are no longer rejected with "microphone couldn't be used."
- **AirPods, honestly.** AirPods are the hardest microphone case in dictation, and this release stops pretending otherwise. Bluetooth makes you choose: the moment anything records from an AirPods mic, the headset drops to its call-quality audio profile, so everything you hear gets worse while you dictate. And accuracy is situational — in a quiet room the Mac's built-in mic transcribes more reliably, while in a noisy place (a plane, a café) the AirPods mic is often the best microphone you own. Automatic switching kept guessing wrong, so speakfree stopped guessing. The compromise: the built-in mic stays the default, and while AirPods are connected a **Dictation Mode** toggle appears in the menu. Turn it on to pin the AirPods mic for noisy rooms — the toggle states the audio-quality cost up front — turn it off and your previous mic comes back.
- **Too noisy to transcribe is now visible.** When the first pass hears nothing in a real take, speakfree rechecks with Whisper and says so, instead of silently typing nothing.
- **Brand and product names stick.** Words you have dictated before, and capitalized names mid-sentence, are no longer overwritten by lookalikes (Cloudflare is no longer "Claude Code").
- **Spoken punctuation is more reliable:** more garbled "comma", "period", "question mark", and "exclamation mark" shapes are repaired, and correctly placed commas are left alone.
- **Record icon adapts to your room.** It no longer flickers on loud-room noise and keeps its contrast on any background.
- **Settings:** the microphone picker lives in Settings with a plain-language note on which mics transcribe reliably; models you have not downloaded are greyed out; the window no longer pauses on first open.
- **Usage stats** show dictations, words, and time, plus keystrokes and typing time saved.
- **Many insertion fixes:** browsers and unlisted Electron apps, Secure Input retry, phantom leading spaces, and a hotkey watchdog that recovers a dead Fn key.
